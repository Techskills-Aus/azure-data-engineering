# Day 01 — Introduction to Data Engineering and the Data Lifecycle

**Module 01 / Chapter 01 of 05**

## Start with the glossary

[Data Engineering and Business Glossary](notes/Data%20Engineering%20and%20Business%20Glossary.md) explains 137 terms in learning order.

## Learning outcomes
1. Define data engineering, its business value, related roles and source contracts.
2. Explain the complete data lifecycle from requirements to governed consumption.
3. Compare ETL/ELT, batch, streaming and CDC at a foundational level.
4. Explain quality gates, idempotency, replay, security, monitoring and ownership.
5. Walk through an Australian retail sales example and a reference Azure design.
